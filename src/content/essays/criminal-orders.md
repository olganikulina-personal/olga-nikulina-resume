## the premise

Some product decisions are crimes — somebody chose, with intention, against the evidence, for reasons they could articulate to a stranger.

Most product decisions are not crimes. They are *orders*. They came down from somewhere; they had a logic that nobody quite owns; they were obviously going to happen as soon as someone said them out loud; and by the time you got the ticket, the decision was a fact, not a choice.

The interesting question is what you do as an engineer in the second case. Not because the order is wrong — usually it's fine — but because the *process* by which it became an order is the only thing you can actually affect, and the thing nobody is asking you to affect.
